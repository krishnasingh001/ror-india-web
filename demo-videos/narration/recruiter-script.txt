This is the ROR World recruiter experience — built for teams hiring Ruby on Rails talent.

Recruiters sign in to a hiring dashboard: posted jobs, applications, and open talent in one view.

They keep their recruiter profile current, then add a company and publish a role in minutes.

My Jobs is where openings are managed — status, applicants, and edits in one list.

On the Applications board, recruiters review candidates on a Kanban pipeline. Open any card to see skills, resume, and profile details, update status, and leave comments for the hiring team.

Then browse the talent marketplace, save strong profiles, and build a shortlist.

ROR World helps recruiters move from posting a Rails job to shortlisting the right developers — faster and with less tooling sprawl.
